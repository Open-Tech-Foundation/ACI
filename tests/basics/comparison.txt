# Standing further along something than another does.

mira is taller than dev
is mira taller than dev? => affirm
is dev shorter than mira? => affirm
is mira shorter than dev? => deny

mira is taller than dev
dev is taller than sam
is mira taller than sam? => affirm
who is the tallest? => mira

a shed is hotter than a hut
a hut is warmer than a barn
is a shed hotter than a barn? => affirm
is a shed warmer than a barn? => affirm

a lake is deeper than a pond
is a lake deeper than a pond? => Yes. ✅ a lake is deeper than a pond.
is a pond shallower than a lake? => Yes. ✅ a pond is shallower than a lake.

# Two things standing in states of one scale compare, with nothing measured.
ravi is tall
kumar is short
is ravi taller than kumar? => affirm
is kumar taller than ravi? => deny
who is taller? => ravi
who is shorter? => kumar
who is tallest? => ravi

# Two at one end of a scale are not one further along than the other.
ravi is tall
kumar is tall
is ravi taller than kumar? => unsure

# Level on a scale, which is not the same as being one thing.
the apple is green
the mango is green
do the apple and the mango have the same colour? => affirm
is the apple the same colour as the mango? => affirm
is the apple the same as the mango? => unsure

the apple is green
the mango is red
do the apple and the mango have the same colour? => deny

# Measured, and asked on the scale the measure is of.
the fence is 8 metres long
the wall is 12 metres long
is the wall longer than the fence? => affirm
is the fence shorter than the wall? => affirm
which is longer, the fence or the wall? => wall

# Units of one scale compare without anything being told.
is an hour longer than a minute? => affirm
is a week longer than a day? => affirm

# More than two, and the far end of them.
devi has 7 ropes
omar has 4 ropes
nila has 9 ropes
who has more ropes? => nila
who has the most ropes? => nila
