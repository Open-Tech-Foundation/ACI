# Standing further along something than another does.

mira is taller than dev
is mira taller than dev? => affirm
is dev shorter than mira? => affirm
is mira shorter than dev? => deny

mira is taller than dev
dev is taller than sam
is mira taller than sam? => affirm
who is the tallest? => mira

a shed is hotter than a hut
a hut is warmer than a barn
is a shed hotter than a barn? => affirm
is a shed warmer than a barn? => affirm

a lake is deeper than a pond
is a lake deeper than a pond? => Yes. ✅ a lake is deeper than a pond.
is a pond shallower than a lake? => Yes. ✅ a pond is shallower than a lake.
