# A claim spoken of rather than made, and what stands behind one.

i know that a mango is a fruit => understood
do i know that a mango is a fruit? => affirm
do i know that a mango is a food? => unsure
who knows that a mango is a fruit? => person

do i know that a mango is a fruit? => unsure
a mango is a fruit? => affirm

i know that a mango is a hammer => deny

the door is open because the wind is strong
why is the door open? => a wind is strong

nila sang because kiran arrived
why did nila sing? => kiran arrived

why is the lamp broken? => unsure
why is a cat => unsure

# A condition and what follows it, said back in words that mean something.
if a ferry is late then the river is rough
the ferry is late
is the river rough? => Yes. ✅ a river is rough.

# Told the consequence does not stand, the condition cannot either.
if a ferry is late then the river is rough
the river is not rough
is the ferry late? => deny

# A choice is not a claim.
the drum is red or the drum is blue
is the drum red? => unsure

# What somebody knows, and what the brain knows.
do you know the ferry is late? => deny
