# What holds between two things, and how it runs.

a wheel is part of a cart
a cart is part of a train
is a wheel part of a train? => affirm
is a train part of a wheel? => deny
is a wheel part of a wheel? => deny

tom is the father of sam
is tom the father of sam? => affirm
who is the father of sam? => tom

mira is the sister of nila
is nila the sibling of mira? => affirm
is nila the sister of mira? => unsure

# Being somebody's father is being their parent, and a parent read from the
# other end is a child. Which sort of child is not something being told about
# the father says, so `son` stays unknown the way `sister` does.
tom is the father of sam
is tom the parent of sam? => affirm
is sam the child of tom? => affirm
is sam the son of tom? => unsure

mira is the mother of kiran
is kiran the child of mira? => affirm

ravi is the husband of nila
is nila the wife of ravi? => affirm

mira is the cousin of nila
is nila the cousin of mira? => affirm

tom is the father of sam
sam is the father of alex
maya is the sister of alex
who is the grandfather of maya? => tom

# A relation runs one way, and asking from the end it runs to is not the same
# question as asking from the end it runs from.
arun is the father of devi
who is the father of devi? => arun
who is the father of arun? => unsure
whose father is arun? => devi

# What a thing is made of, it has; and what it has need not be part of it.
the wheel is part of the cart
is the wheel part of the cart? => affirm
does the cart have a wheel? => affirm
what does the cart have? => wheel

devi has 3 ropes
is a rope part of devi? => unsure

# What a thing is part of is not something it has.
the wheel is part of the cart
the cart is part of the train
what does the cart have? => wheel
does the train have a wheel? => affirm

# The long way round says whose it is.
the roof of the shed is red
what colour is the roof? => red
is the shed red? => unsure
what does the shed have? => roof

# A relation the world declares a converse for answers from either end.
mira is the mother of kiran
is kiran the child of mira? => affirm
