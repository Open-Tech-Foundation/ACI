# How much of something there is.

the mast is 6 metres long
how long is the mast? => six metres

the plank is 1 metre long
how long is the plank? => one metre

the crate weighs 5 kilograms
how many grams does the crate weigh? => five thousand
how many kilograms does the crate weigh? => five

a shelf holds 4 stamps
how many stamps does the shelf hold? => four
how many grams does the shelf hold? => unsure

# Said of one shelf twice, the later count is the one it holds. Said of `a`
# shelf twice, that is two shelves, and which of them is being asked after is
# not something the brain is in a position to guess.
a shelf holds 4 stamps
the shelf holds 6 stamps
how many stamps does the shelf hold? => six

a shelf holds 4 stamps
a shelf holds 6 stamps
how many stamps does a shelf hold? => unsure

# A rope is long and a lecture is long, and the two are not the same longness.
# Which quantity is meant is the one the unit reads.
the lecture lasted three hours
how long was the lecture? => three hours

the rope is 2 metres long
how long is the rope? => two metres

# A measure told, asked back, and converted.
the rope is 3 metres long
how long is the rope? => three metres
the ladder is 5 metres long
is the ladder longer than the rope? => affirm

# A scale with no units answers with the state.
the ball is small
what size is the ball? => small

# Asked how much of something nothing was measured in.
the ball is small
how long is the ball? => unsure
