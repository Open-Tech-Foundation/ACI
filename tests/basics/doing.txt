# A doing said with a hole, asked from either side of it.
devi carried the ladder
who carried the ladder? => devi

what did devi carry? => ladder

# A doing and what it brought about.
the door is closed
ravi opened the door
is the door open? => affirm
was the door closed? => affirm
why is the door open? => ravi opened a door

# A doing named as a thing happened.
a plank fell after a meeting
did the meeting happen? => affirm
did a party happen? => unsure
what happened first? => meeting
what happened last? => plank

# One doing inside another.
nila spoke during the meeting
who was in the meeting? => nila

# A told cause is a cause.
the storm delayed the ferry
why was the ferry delayed? => a storm

# Each side of an ordering keeps its own doing.

did the dog run? => affirm
did the child sing? => affirm
did the child run? => unsure
what happened first? => child
