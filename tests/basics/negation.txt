# Being told something is not so is being told something.

a wren is not a fish
is a wren a fish? => deny

a bell is not red
is a bell red? => deny

is a bell blue? => unsure

# A denial is a denial whatever it is said of. A relation denied is as much a
# telling as a property denied, and the brain holds both the same way.
omar is the father of devi
is omar the father of devi? => affirm

omar is not the father of devi
is omar the father of devi? => deny

the coin is not in the jar
is the coin in the jar? => deny

# Denied, and then asked the other way round: what does not stand does not
# stand from either end.
the wheel is not part of the cart
is the wheel part of the cart? => deny

# A denial says nothing about anything else.
a wren is not a fish
is a wren a bird? => affirm

# And a kind the world keeps apart from another is denied without being told.
is a heron a fish? => deny

# Asked why something is not so, where it was told that it is not.
the drum is not cold
why is the drum not cold? => unsure
