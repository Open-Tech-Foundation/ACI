# Where a thing is, and how two of them stand to each other.

the lamp is on the crate
where is the lamp? => on a crate
what is on the crate? => lamp

the lamp is on the crate
the lamp is in the sack
where is the lamp? => in a sack

a lamp is left of a crate
a crate is left of a barrel
is a lamp left of a barrel? => affirm
is a barrel left of a lamp? => deny
is a barrel right of a lamp? => affirm
is a lamp left of a lamp? => deny

x is left of y
y is left of z
x is left of z? => affirm

# Where a thing is, and what is there.
the coin is in the jar
where is the coin? => in a jar
what is in the jar? => coin
what is in the coin? => unsure

# Containment carries.
the coin is in the jar
the jar is on the shelf
where is the coin? => in a jar
is the coin on the shelf? => affirm

# A doing moves a thing, and where it was is behind it.
the coin is in the jar
devi put the coin on the shelf
where is the coin? => on a shelf
was the coin in the jar? => affirm
